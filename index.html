Table chat

Official-style rules: match color or symbol. After your second-to-last card is played, say UNO! before the next player moves, or anyone can catch you for +2. Drawn playable cards can be played right away. Optional +2/+4 stacking is chosen before each game. Cards are hidden on screen but not truly secret from technical snooping, so play with friends you trust.
